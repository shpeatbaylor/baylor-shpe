Who We Are

Baylor SHPE is a student organization dedicated to supporting Hispanic students pursuing careers in engineering, technology, science, and related fields. Through academic support, professional development, service, and community, we help our members grow both personally and professionally.

Baylor SHPE

What We Do

Academic Development

We create opportunities for members to study, collaborate, and strengthen their academic skills.

Professional Development

Members gain access to networking opportunities, career preparation, company events, and professional resources.

Community

We build a strong familia where students can form lasting friendships and meaningful connections.

Service

Our members give back to Baylor and the surrounding community through outreach and volunteer opportunities.
